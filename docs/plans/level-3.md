# Level 3: the residential sector (step 4 after 3.0)

Status: a direction, not a design (2026-10-05); fourth of the six steps
after 3.0 (`ROADMAP.md`). The first level built in the editor
(`level-editor.md`) and the first wide one (`wide-frame.md`). Nothing
built; the lore is `lore/LORE.md`, section 9, item 3.

## What it is
Quarters, the mess hall, the greenhouse. An empty home is scarier than
an empty factory; the "residential mini-complex" sign of level 1 comes
true here. Mechanically gentle - the entry into phase 2. Its new code is
the wide screen itself.

## Decided so far
- **The look** (2026-10-05): the design is not restricted. The loading
  station was rusty, so the city is a bit Tron-like - modern, retrowave -
  yet the place must feel creepy. The concept accepted for now, still
  being thought over: a home still waiting for its residents; dread
  without a new alien faction.
- **The opening** (2026-10-05): the level starts with an incident -
  something falls, the satellite of level 1 for instance, the way the
  plane falls at the start of the first Duke Nukem level.
- **The boss** (the ladder of 2026-10-02): at least twice the size of the
  level 1 wheel; the bosses grow gradually from here, giants on levels
  5-6.
- Levels 1-2 are not touched for it.
